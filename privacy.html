Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026 · Last updated: June 1, 2026

Few Days ("the App") is a personal app for tracking time with loved ones. Your privacy matters deeply to us.

What data we collect

Nothing. Few Days collects no personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry of any kind.

  • No account required
  • No network connections
  • No third-party SDKs or analytics
  • No advertising identifiers

Where your data is stored

All information you enter (names, ages, check-in records) is stored locally on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data never leaves your device.

Data deletion

Deleting the App removes all data permanently. There is no cloud backup.

Children

Few Days is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Given that the App collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.
